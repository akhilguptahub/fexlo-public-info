You stay in control

  • You choose the websites. Bulk Fetch reads only the sites you add.
  • Chrome asks for access to each site when it is needed, one site at a time, after you click. You can withdraw it in Chrome’s extension settings.
  • Product datasets are stored in your browser, not in Fexlo’s account service.
  • Account details (name, email, organisation and your sign-in) are sent to Supabase, our account service provider, so your account works.
  • Fexlo respects robots.txt and stops when a site refuses access (403), asks it to slow down (429) or shows a bot check.
  • Fexlo never solves CAPTCHAs and does not try to get past bot protection.

Which sources work

Fexlo reads stores that publish a public Shopify-style product list, and product lists on the page you are viewing, on a best-effort basis. It does not work on every store or platform, and it does not claim compatibility with any specific store until that has been tested. Read compatibility and limits before relying on a result.

Questions

What does Fexlo collect?

Your account details (name, email, an optional organisation, and the password you sign in with) are sent to our account service, Supabase. Product data, your website lists, filters and batches stay in your browser. The privacy policy lists each item.

Which websites are supported?

Stores that publish a public Shopify-style product list, and product lists on the page you are viewing, on a best-effort basis. Fexlo does not work on every store or platform. See compatibility and limits.

Where is product data stored?

In your browser, in the extension’s own storage on your device. It is not uploaded to Fexlo’s account service. Remove the extension, or delete data in the Library, to remove it.

Why does Chrome ask for site access?

Chrome requires permission before an extension can read a website. Fexlo asks for each website you add in Bulk Fetch, one at a time, after you click. You can withdraw access in Chrome’s extension settings.

What happens when a site blocks access?

Fexlo marks that site as blocked when it refuses access (403), asks it to slow down (429) or shows a bot check. It stops reading that site, does not try to get past the block, and the other sites in the batch continue.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Results and saved datasets can be exported as CSV or JSON. Each export records the source and capture time of the data.

Is Fexlo free?

Fexlo is free during Early Access. Future pricing has not been announced, so this is not a promise that it will stay free.

Where can I get help?

Read the install and help guide, then contact support using the address on the support page. Include the Fexlo version (Settings, then About) and what the screen said.
