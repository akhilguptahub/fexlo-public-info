What it does

  • Turns a product list on the page you are viewing into a clean table you can export.
  • Reads a store's public catalogue (products, variants, prices as published) politely and slowly.
  • Bulk Fetch: paste or import several websites, choose which products you want from each, allow access once, and fetch them all with one click — with per-site progress, pause, resume, stop and retry.
  • Keeps a Library on your device, compares snapshots, exports CSV or JSON.

Honest by design

  • It obeys robots.txt and stops when a site refuses (403), asks it to slow down (429) or shows a bot check. It never solves CAPTCHAs.
  • Each website in a batch needs your permission, asked one site at a time. Fexlo has no website access when you install it.
  • Only your account details go to our account service. Products, website lists and filters never do. See the privacy policy.
  • Results are labelled truthfully: completed, completed with warnings, partial, skipped, blocked or failed.
